Checks worth making when the weather turns

These are general habits and not rules for Port Norris. Adjust them to your property.

  • After a hard rain, note any drain that slows or gurgles and whether the yard stays soggy.
  • Find your main water shutoff now and make sure everyone in the house can reach it.
  • Look at exposed pipes in unheated spaces before a cold stretch and insulate what you can.
  • Check that the cleanout cap is in place, since a missing cap lets water and debris in.
  • Watch the water meter or bill for a rise that has no clear cause.
  • Keep the number for a septic or well contact next to the phone.

Public water, private well, or something else?

Do not assume. The Commercial Township site does not say whether your street has public water and sewer, so confirm it with the township before planning a repair. The answer decides whether you call a utility, a well contractor or us.

If you have a private well, New Jersey has a Private Well Testing Act through the state environmental department. The state lists wells built before 1996, hand-dug wells and wells whose level drops when nearby surface water drops as higher risk. A household with that kind of well should ask about testing before changing plumbing that touches the water supply.
