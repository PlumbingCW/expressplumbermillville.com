Which utility handles what

Municipal boundaries decide who runs water and sewer. Millville and Vineland each have their own water utility, and Bridgeton has a city Division of Water and Sewer. In Port Norris and the rest of Commercial Township, confirm with the township whether a property is on public water or sewer or on a well and septic system.

That matters because it decides who fixes what. Problems in the street belong to the utility, and problems on your side of the connection belong to you.

Cannot tell whether it is the city or you?

A quick check usually answers it. If only your home has the problem, the cause is most likely on your side of the connection. If neighbors have no water, low pressure or sewage in the street, the city utility needs to hear about it first.

Details that help us route your call

Having these ready gets the right help to the right address faster.

  • The full street address and the nearest cross street, which matters most in the smaller communities around Millville.
  • Which fixture or pipe is affected, and whether the problem is one fixture or the whole house.
  • Whether the main water valve is already off and whether the water stopped when you closed it.
  • Whether neighbors are affected, which tells us if the problem may be on the street side.
  • Whether the property is on city water and sewer or on a private well and septic system.
  • Any smell of gas or sewage, which changes what we tell you to do before anyone arrives.
