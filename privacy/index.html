Privacy Policy

Overview

This policy explains how Millville Express Plumbing handles information when you visit this website or call our phone line. We keep it short because we collect very little. Effective date: October 23, 2026.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address and a description of the plumbing problem so we can arrange service.

We use that information only to respond to your request and manage the job. We do not sell it.

Website information

This site has no forms and no accounts. Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics tools, if enabled, may collect basic technical data such as browser type and pages viewed.

The site embeds Google Maps and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may set their own cookies or receive your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Email

If you email contact@expressplumbermillville.com, we keep the message so we can reply and keep a record of the conversation.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@expressplumbermillville.com.
