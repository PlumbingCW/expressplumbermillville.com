Before the truck gets to your Vineland address

These steps limit damage while you wait for us. Do them only if you can reach the spot safely.

Close the main water valve

The main shutoff is usually near where the line enters the house or beside the meter. If you cannot find it, say so on the call and we will help you look.

Keep clear of water and power

If water is near outlets, a panel or a plugged-in appliance, do not touch them. Turn off the breaker only if you can reach it while standing on dry ground.

A gas smell means leave

Walk outside, then call 911 and your gas utility from there. Do not flip switches or use a lighter on the way out.

Open a low faucet and clear the floor

A tap at the lowest point of the house helps drain what is left in a ruptured line. Lift rugs and boxes off the wet area so the damage stays small.

A planned city with a very large footprint

Vineland began in 1861 as a planned community, and buyers had to build within a year. It became a city in 1952, when Landis Township and Vineland Borough merged. Today it covers 68.4 square miles of land, the largest city type municipality in New Jersey by area.
