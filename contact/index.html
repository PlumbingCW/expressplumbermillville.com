The fastest way to reach us is to call

Plumbing problems rarely wait for office hours, so the 24/7 emergency line is the best way to reach us. Call and tell us what is happening, and we will help you stop the damage while we arrange the visit.

We do not use online forms. A phone call gets you a real conversation, a clear price before work starts and, when available, a same-day appointment.

What to have ready when you call

A few details help us help you faster.

  1. Your addressStreet and town, so we know if you are in Millville or one of the nearby towns we serve.
  2. What you seeWhere the water is, how fast it is moving and which fixture or pipe it comes from.
  3. What you already didWhether the main valve is off, and whether anything else stopped working.
  4. Anything unsafeA gas smell or water near electrical outlets. Gas means leave the building and call 911 first.

Where we work

We take calls by phone and serve Millville and nearby towns. There is no walk-in office, so call first and we will talk through what is happening and what comes next.

What happens after you call

You reach the dispatch line, and we ask a few short questions about the address, the symptom and what you have already done. If water is running, we walk you through stopping it first.

Next we agree on the visit. If the problem is urgent, we say what we can do and how soon the schedule allows, without promising a set arrival time. Before any work starts, you get a clear price, and you decide whether to go ahead.

While you wait for us

If water is moving, these four steps limit the damage.

Shut off the main

Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. Know where it is before you need it.

Protect electrics

If water is near outlets or appliances, do not touch them. Switch off power only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.

Move belongings

Lift rugs, boxes and electronics away from the water, and open a few drawers of towels as a dam if needed.

Take photos

Photograph the damage and the failed part for your insurance claim before cleanup.

When to email instead

Email suits non-urgent questions, such as asking which service fits a problem or whether we cover your street. Write to contact@expressplumbermillville.com and describe what you see.

For anything involving active water, a backup or no hot water, calling is faster. Email is not monitored the way the emergency line is.

Utility numbers worth knowing

If the problem looks like it sits on the street side of your connection, the city may need to handle it. Millville's Water Utility has its own emergency line, and the city asks residents to call the Sewer Utility first for drain problems. Both are reachable through City Hall at 12 South High Street.

Call us for anything on your property, and we will tell you straight away if the city needs to be involved.

Before a planned visit

Not every call is an emergency. For installs, upgrades and non-urgent repairs, a little preparation keeps the visit short.

  • Clear a path to the water heater, under-sink area or panel where the work will happen.
  • Note the make and model of any fixture or appliance being replaced, if you can see it on a label.
  • Tell us if anyone in the home needs water on at a particular time so we can plan the shutoff.
  • Mention pets, gates or parking limits so the visit starts without delay.
  • Ask about permits for the job and check the rules with the Millville Construction Office.

Planned work and the emergency line

The emergency line is answered at every hour, and planned work is booked around your schedule. Tell us on the call whether the job is urgent or can wait for a day that suits you.

We offer same-day appointments when available, so even a non-urgent call is worth making early in the day.
