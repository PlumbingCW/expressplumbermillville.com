Bridgeton's Division of Water and Sewer and Chapter 360

Water and sewer service in Bridgeton runs through the City of Bridgeton Division of Water and Sewer under city code Chapter 360. The code lets officials enter a property to inspect pipes connected to the supply facility. Unpaid water charges become a lien on the property, so buyers and sellers should ask about account status before closing. Owners planning pipe work tied to the city line should call the Division first.

Septic is a separate track. Bridgeton falls under the Cumberland County Department of Health, so a property with a septic system should ask the county about inspection and repair steps.

Source type: City of Bridgeton code Chapter 360 on eCode360 and Cumberland County septic management plan.

If water or sewage is already coming in

Do the safe things first and let us handle the rest.

Stop the supply

Turn off the main water valve. If a single fixture is the source, close the stop valve under it and leave the main alone.

Do not run drains

If sewage is backing up, stop using sinks, tubs and toilets. Every flush adds to what is already coming back.

Treat wet floors as live

Do not walk through standing water near outlets or a panel. Call a utility or an electrician if the basement is flooded around electrical equipment.

Smell gas, go outside

Leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility from outside. Do not turn lights on or off.
